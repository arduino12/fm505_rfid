Settings

TX power (N1)

−2 to 25 dBm. Lower power shortens range, which helps when you only want tags right at the antenna.

Region / frequency band (N5)

The antenna is tuned for 902–928 MHz. Use the band that is allowed where you operate.

Module UART baud rate (NA)

The page reconnects at the new rate. If contact is lost, pick the rate in the top bar.

GPIO (N6–N9)
PinOutputHigh

Inputs show the level last read; outputs take the High setting on Apply.
